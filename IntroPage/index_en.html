ABOUT ELEMENTALISM

Elementalism is an innovative deck-building roguelike puzzle game where players strategically place elemental blocks on a 9x9 grid to activate powerful card abilities. Inspired by hits like Slay the Spire and Peglin, it combines tactical combat with engaging puzzle mechanics for a fresh gaming experience.

Your journey takes you through procedurally generated battles, events, and rewards. Collect relics to enhance your abilities, build the perfect elemental deck, and defeat the final boss to claim victory!

GAME FEATURES

Elemental Puzzle Battles

Place Tetris-like blocks on the 9x9 grid to match shapes on your cards. Each element (Fire, Water, Earth, Air) has unique properties that influence your strategy.

Strategic Deck-Building

Collect and upgrade cards with diverse abilities. Craft a synergistic deck that complements your playstyle and the elemental blocks you'll encounter.

Powerful Relics

Discover artifacts that dramatically alter your capabilities. Each relic provides unique bonuses that can turn the tide of battle in your favor.

Endless Replayability

Randomized elements, events, and enemy encounters ensure no two playthroughs are the same. Adapt your strategy each time you play!

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

Turn Structure

  1. 1 Choose one of three elemental blocks (Fire, Water, Earth, Air)
  2. 2 Place the block on the 12x5 puzzle grid (similar to Tetris mechanics)
  3. 3 Any cards that match the placed block's pattern are activated, triggering their effects
  4. 4 Enemies perform their actions (attacking, special abilities, or approaching)

Progression System

Between battles, you'll navigate through nodes on a world map, choosing your path strategically:

Battles

Defeat enemies to earn new cards and relics

Shops

Spend collected currency on upgrades

Events

Make choices with lasting consequences

Boss

Test your skills against powerful foes

Strategy Tips

  • Plan several moves ahead to chain powerful card combos
  • Balance your deck between different elements for flexibility
  • Pay attention to enemy patterns and position blocks to counter them
  • Some relics synergize perfectly with specific elemental cards
